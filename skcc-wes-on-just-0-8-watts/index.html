SKCC WES on Just 0.8 Watts

November 15, 2026

What Is SKCC WES?

If you enjoy Morse code, you should give the SKCC Weekend Sprintathon (WES) a try. It's a fun on-air event that happens once a month, put on by the Straight Key Century Club. It's friendly, relaxed, and a great excuse to dust off your straight key, bug, or cootie (sideswiper) and make some contacts. You don't have to be fast, and plenty of folks are happy to slow down and work you.

Check skccgroup.com for the dates, rules, and each month's theme.

Becky and Mike on 0.8 Watts

This month, Becky and I both took part using a little experimental radio from Barb WB2CBA. Barb designed the Pebble HF, which we collaborated with her on. This new radio puts out only 0.8 watts, similar to the Pebble HF when it's powered from USB, but it has a unique design of its own.

It has a straight key built right in, which makes it about as simple as radio gets. And it absolutely blew us away. We heard so many great signals, and they were easy to copy. We made contacts all across the country with less than one watt!
